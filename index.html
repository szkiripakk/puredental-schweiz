Font usage guide

  • Hero / display700–800 weight, maximum 2–3 lines, tight letter spacing.
  • Section headings700 weight, sentence case, one clear message per heading.
  • Body copy400–500 weight, 65–72 characters per line, comfortable 1.6–1.75 line height.
  • Navigation500–600 weight, 14–15 px, short labels only.
  • Buttons600–700 weight, 15–16 px, action-focused wording.
  • Eyebrows700 weight, uppercase, generous tracking, used sparingly.
