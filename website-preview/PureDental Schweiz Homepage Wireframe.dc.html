08 · THE WRITTEN GUARANTEE

Promises in writing, not in conversation

The Swiss Dental Association warns about clinics abroad with vague guarantees. We agree with them. Here's ours, in detail.

A guarantee that lives only in a brochure is no guarantee at all. Ours is a real legal document, signed and provided as a PDF before any treatment begins. It specifies guarantee periods per treatment type, the conditions under which it applies, and exactly what happens if something needs to be redone. We even cover up to CHF 300 of your travel costs if a warranty issue requires you to return to Budapest.

TREATMENT TYPEPERIODNOTES
Premium Straumann implantsUp to 10 yearsLifetime on the implant body, 10 years on prosthetic components
Standard implants5 yearsOn both the implant and the abutment
Zirconium crowns and bridges5 yearsAesthetic and structural integrity
Metal-ceramic crowns and bridges3 yearsFunction and structural integrity
E-max veneers3 yearsSubject to standard wear conditions
Root canal treatment2 yearsTreatment success and tooth viability

Annual follow-up at our Swiss consultation room is required to maintain warranty. Standard exclusions apply for trauma, neglect, and tobacco use on certain treatments — all listed clearly in the PDF, no fine print.
